Information Security at a Big 4 global consulting firm and an authorized ISC2
instructor teaching CISSP, CCSP, and AI strategy.

My background spans offensive security, red teaming, penetration testing, and operational technology (OT) security,
anchored by deep expertise in security architecture, incident response advisory,
and cyber project management. With a current focus on how AI is reshaping both
attack and defense, I bridge the gap between tactical execution and strategic
resilience.

I also build open-source security tooling and write about what I learn. This
site is where I share tools, writeups, and analysis.
