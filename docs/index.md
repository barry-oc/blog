## About

I'm a cybersecurity leader based in Tokyo, Japan. I'm a Senior Manager in
Cyber & Information Security at PwC, and an authorized ISC2 instructor teaching
CISSP, CCSP, and AI strategy. My background spans offensive security, red
teaming, penetration testing, and vulnerability assessment — with a current
focus on how AI is reshaping both attack and defense.

I also build open-source security tooling and write about what I learn. This
site is where I share tools, writeups, and analysis — no hype, no vendor fluff.

## What I focus on

- **Offensive AI security** — how threat actors and red teams weaponise AI
- **Red teaming & penetration testing** — application and infrastructure testing
- **Security leadership** — strategy, governance, and international delivery
- **Education** — developing the next generation (CISSP, CCSP, AI strategy)
