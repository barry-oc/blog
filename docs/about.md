# About

I'm a cybersecurity professional based in Japan. I build tools, break things
(for a living), and write about what I learn along the way.

## What I do

**Tooling** — I publish open-source security tools, primarily in Python.
Check out [my projects](/projects/) or [GitHub](https://github.com/barry-oc).

**Research** — I dig into CVEs, protocols, and attack surfaces, and I write
up my findings on this blog.

**Blue & purple team** — I work on detection engineering, automation, and
making defenders' lives easier.

## Philosophy

Security is practical, not performative. I care about tools that actually
solve problems, writeups that teach something real, and advice that doesn't
require a $50K budget to follow.

If a tool or technique can't be explained to a junior engineer in five
minutes, it's not done yet.

## This blog

This is where I document things I build, things I break, and things I think
are worth sharing. No hot takes, no vendor hype — just honest technical
content. [Start reading →](/blog/)
